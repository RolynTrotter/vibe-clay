🏺 vibe-clay

Glaze chemistry in your pocket · a companion to Insight-Live

Recipe Library

Export your library from Insight-Live (XML) and open it here — every recipe, with chemistry, on your phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; parsing happens on your device.

Analysis

Unity Formula (UMF)

Fluxes (blue) sum to 1.0. Al₂O₃ / Fe₂O₃ are stabilisers (amber), B₂O₃ boron (purple), SiO₂ & other glass-formers (green). Expansion is a relative additive estimate for comparing glazes, not a dilatometer value.

Line Blend

Blend two glazes across a line and see the UMF & analysis at every point — the classic way to explore the space between a glossy and a matte, or a base and a variant. Pick Glaze A, Glaze B, and how many points.

Paste a Recipe

Copy a recipe from Insight-Live, a book, or a forum post and paste it here — one material and amount per line. A line reading “Additions” or “Colorants” puts everything below it in the additions group, or start a line with +.

Import / Export

Export XML gives you an Insight-Live-shaped file for the current recipe — import it back into Insight-Live to get it into your account. Import reads either JSON or Insight-Live XML.
